# Privacy

cheapshot does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The app makes no network connections.
- Screenshots, recordings and recognized text stay on your Mac. They go only where you put them: the clipboard, a folder you pick or a file you save.
- Text recognition runs on your Mac, with Apple's Vision framework.
- Settings, and a log with a summary of each recording's audio, are kept in the app's own folder on your Mac, `~/Library/Containers/com.andremorata.cheapshot`. Deleting the app and that folder removes them.

cheapshot asks for Screen Recording permission to capture and record the screen, and for Microphone permission to record your voice. It uses them only for the captures and recordings you start.

Questions go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/andremorata/cheapshot/issues).
